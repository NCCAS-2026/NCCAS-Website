ASTRONOMICAL HERITAGE

A COMMUNITY CONNECTED BY THE COSMOS.

Welcome to the Astronomy Society of A/Niwaththakachethiya National College. We are dedicated to nurturing scientific curiosity, observational acumen, and hands-on astronomical discovery among young scholars.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate, and empower the students of A/Niwaththakachethiya National College by demystifying astronomy, facilitating regular telescope skywatch camps, and offering foundational training in observational techniques and modern astrophysics.

Our Vision

To establish NCCAS as a premier student astronomy hub in the North Central Province and across Sri Lanka, recognized for developing scientifically grounded thinkers who gaze at the night sky with wonder, critical insight, and academic rigor.

Why Astronomy Matters

Astronomy is a humbling and character-building experience. It sparks cross-disciplinary curiosity spanning physics, mathematics, computing, and optics, reminding every student of our shared responsibility as custodians of planet Earth.

FLAGSHIP PROVINCIAL CAMPAIGN

ASTRONOMICAL

ASTRONOMICAL is NCCAS's signature initiative dedicated to expanding space science literacy, telescope observational workshops, and academic collaboration across schools in Anuradhapura and the North Central Province.

Stargazing Nights

Equipping students with optical telescopes to track Saturn, Jupiter, and deep-sky nebulae in real time under pristine night skies.

Space Workshops

Practical laboratory sessions on telescope alignment, focal calibration, astrophotography mounts, and solar filtering.

Astroscience Quizzes

Inter-grade challenges encouraging students to delve into orbital mechanics, planetary geology, and cosmic history.

Citizen Research

Introductory asteroid tracking and celestial image processing using open astronomical survey archives.
